# Fameverse Pro Live Achievements — Future Roadmap

Status: future roadmap only. The current app may show a read-only teaser, but no eligibility, badge assignment, tracking, enforcement, or creator rewards are active yet.

## Planned achievement titles

### Fameverse Silver Pro Live
- Requirement: accumulate 100,000 Fame Coins per week.
- Grace rule: one missed week is allowed. The following week is the final chance to meet the requirement before Silver is lost.

### Fameverse Gold Pro Live
- Requirement: accumulate 500,000 Fame Coins per month.
- Grace rule: one missed month is allowed. The following month is the final chance to meet the requirement before Gold is lost.

### Fameverse Diamond Pro Live
- Requirement: accumulate 1,000,000 Fame Coins per month.
- Grace rule: none. Missing a single monthly requirement loses Diamond status.

## Current-candidate law
- The feature is informational/read-only only.
- The app reveals only a small teaser (about 10%) and visually blurs the unreleased details.
- No database tables, counters, automatic qualification, badge grants, status loss, or rewards are implemented in the current candidate.
- Basics and stability remain the release priority before this feature is activated.
