# Fameverse Law — Branded Cinematic Gift Presentation
**Status:** OWNER REQUESTED, review-only. Issue: #62.

1. Every delivered moving/cinematic gift carries the existing *approved Fameverse watermark* in its render-safe zone. One watermark per event, consistent across native Flutter and PWA. Do not invent a replacement brand identity. Branding may be placed in the presentation layer; do not re-encode or alter an original approved video/audio asset just to add a watermark without owner creative review.
2. **No visible subtitles, underlines, default system emotes or second emoji/label immediately underneath cinematic gift media.** Gift sender identity and official activity/gift chat bubble can remain in the approved separate Live activity surface. Accessibility labels are not visible subtitles.
3. Video and audio start and finish as the approved asset intends, with no forced 2x loop, overlapping duplicate audio/video, background player restart, black/stale frame, clipped tail, or source mute substitution. Do not trim the original media to match a shorter UI indicator timer.
4. Under-100 coin gifts use lightweight presentation, no cinematic premium audio/video. Dragon/Phoenix stay actual approved media/poster art at owner-approved pricing; Pocket Comet stays retired. No emoji stand-ins for cinematic media.
5. Verify watermark contrast and safe positioning on small iPhones, host/viewer/cohost, PWA Safari; title/subtitle rule, one gift per accepted event, queued combo playback, original sound end, recovery on media errors and reusable genuine poster frames. Audio/image tests require real devices; static tests alone cannot certify visual playback.

Any changes to specific gift creative media files, titles, timing, names or pricing beyond the stated brand overlay require separate owner review. Separate PRs for PWA and Flutter. Never merge or distribute before UI snapshot/video preview and physical QA.
